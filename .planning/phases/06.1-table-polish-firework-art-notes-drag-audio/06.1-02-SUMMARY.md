# Phase 6.1 Plan 02: Firework Burst Art Summary

Reworked `suit-visuals.ts` in place to replace Phase 6's geometric glyphs with seven original, hand-authored firework-burst silhouettes (pairwise distinct with colour ignored), added rank-count burst layouts and a neutral card-back art descriptor, and exposed them through new `FireworkCardFace`/`FireworkCardBack` components plus a dev-only `/dev/fireworks` preview page for the owner's pre-build sign-off (D-31).

## What Was Built

**Task 1 — Burst silhouettes, rank layouts, card-back art (`suit-visuals.ts`):** Added two pure path-generator helpers, `starBurstPath` (alternating outer/inner vertex star polygon, outer radius may be a single number or an array cycled across vertices for asymmetric spikes) and `ringBurstPath` (a star polygon plus a circular evenodd hole), and used them to build all seven `glyphPath` values at module load time: red (12-spike uniform chrysanthemum), yellow (16-spike dense round-petal), green (10-spike hollow ring, evenodd), blue (8-spike asymmetric 4-long/4-short cross-star), white (6-spike sparse sparkler), rainbow (20-spike largest single-tone burst), black (compact double hollow ring, evenodd). Each `SuitVisual` gained `fillRule` and a `silhouette: { spikes, rings, hollow }` descriptor; all seven descriptor tuples are pairwise distinct. Added `burstLayoutForRank(rank)` returning box-game-style unit-coordinate placements (1 centre, 2 diagonal, 3 diagonal line, 4 corners, 5 corners+centre) and `CARD_BACK_ART` (a 4-layer neutral card-back motif — border frame, surface fill, faint shell-diamond emblem, soft top glow arc — coloured only via `var(--color-border)`, `var(--color-surface)`, `var(--color-text-muted)`, `var(--color-card-glow)`). `SuitGlyph.tsx` now passes `fillRule` through to its rendered `<path>`.

**Task 2 — `FireworkCardFace`/`FireworkCardBack` and dev preview:** `FireworkCardFace` maps `burstLayoutForRank(rank)` (or a single centred burst when `showBurstCount` is false) to absolutely positioned `SuitGlyph` instances plus a corner rank numeral (`--text-body` or `--text-label` per the `numeralSize` prop); the `data-glyph` identity marker is emitted only on the outer wrapper, never forwarded into the inner bursts. `FireworkCardBack` accepts only `width`/`height` — no suit, rank, or facts prop exists on its signature, so it is structurally incapable of leaking identity — and renders `CARD_BACK_ART`'s layers as SVG paths. `app/dev/fireworks/page.tsx` is a server component that calls `notFound()` when `NODE_ENV === "production"`, otherwise rendering every suit glyph at 5 sizes, every suit's card faces at ranks 1-5 across three sizes (64x84, 48x64 with the burst-count-vs-numeral-only fallback shown side by side, 88x112), the card back at two sizes, and the entire grid repeated under `filter: grayscale(1)` for colourblind-safe review.
