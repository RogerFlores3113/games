<objective>
Write Expedition's Zod wire schemas in `packages/schema/src/games/`: the strict per-seat view schema (field-for-field mirror of Plan 11-01's `ExpeditionView` contract), the closed error-code enum, and the no-settings config schema, plus the `@games/schema/games/expedition` subpath wiring.

Purpose: The worker validates every outbound game view against its registry entry's `viewSchema` (`seat-projection.ts` `validateGameView`, fail-closed). A strict schema is the second, independent layer of COMM-03's leak defense: even a projection bug that adds a key is refused on the wire. This plan deliberately does NOT widen `GameIdSchema`, `GameErrorDetailSchema` or `CreateRoomRequestSchema` — those edits break worker/web typecheck until the registry entry exists, so they land together with it in Plan 11-06.

Output: expedition.ts, expedition-errors.ts, expedition.test.ts, subpath wiring in package.json exports / tsconfig.base.json paths / vitest.config.ts aliases.
</objective>
