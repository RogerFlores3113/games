<objective>
Implement objective evaluation for all four spec §5.2 kinds as an ObjectiveKindDef-shaped registry, plus the pure clockwise objective-pick sequencing function. Every evaluator recomputes status fresh from CampState (never reads or writes a stored status), and detects impossibility at the earliest trick the spec's rule text allows.

Purpose: XRULE-06 (four kinds, status always computable), XRULE-07 (instant failure detection at the objective level), XRULE-05 (clockwise pick order from the leader).
Output: objectives.ts and objectives.test.ts. Plan 04's checkCampOutcome consumes objectiveStatuses; Plan 06 adds the fast-check failure-timing proof.

Resolutions of RESEARCH open questions for this plan (spec is silent; each recorded as a labeled assumption in objectives.ts's header comment and in must_haves for owner review):
- Open Question 2 (two ordered cards won in the same trick) → A-TIE: same trick satisfies order (non-strict). Rationale: neither card was won strictly after the other; this is the more permissive reading and changing it later is a one-comparator edit.
- §5.2 "last" marker → A-LAST: literal reading of "or not in the last trick".
- Research A4 → A-END: trick-count objectives resolve to done only at camp end (a holder could still win more tricks until then).
- Open Question 3 (holder reassignment) → evaluators read ownerSeatId at call time and derive trick counts from completedTricks, so a Phase 10 swap needs no new fields.
- Open Question 5 → A-MULTI (declared in state.ts): picking wraps clockwise; a seat may hold several objectives.
</objective>
