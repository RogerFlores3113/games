<objective>
Close the engine half of UAT gap 3 ("black is reverse - there's 3x 5s, 2x of 4 3 2, and 1x 1s, and you play them in reverse order - 5 then 4 then 3 then 2 then 1"). Black becomes a descending suit with a 10-tile descending copy distribution, driven by a `direction` field on the suit's rule in the variant configuration. The engine's stack model changes from `topRank` to `playedRanks`.

DESIGN DECISION (binding for 07-10 and 07-11). Stack progress is represented as `playedRanks: Rank[]`, the ranks on the stack in the order they were played (Black: [5, 4, ...]; red: [1, 2, ...]). This applies to the engine `StackEntry` here and to the wire `StackView` in 07-11. Direction lives only in the variant configuration (`SuitRule.direction`), and the engine reaches it through `playOrderFor(config, suit)` / `nextPlayableRank(config, stack)`. Completion is `playedRanks.length === RANKS.length`, and a stack's score contribution is `playedRanks.length`. A reversed stack's "top" is never read as a number, so nothing can mistake a falling rank for progress. In this plan `projection.ts` keeps filling the existing wire field `topRank` with `playedRanks.length` (the tile count, identical to today's value for every ascending stack). The web client and its tests therefore stay green at every commit, and 07-11 replaces the wire field with `playedRanks` in the same task as its web consumers.

Purpose: the engine must play Black the way the owner plays it.
Output: a direction-aware variant table, a 70-tile Black deck, a direction-aware engine (play legality, completion, clue-token refund, score, conservation helpers), ROOM_SCHEMA_VERSION 4, and every test that assumed ascending-only stacks or a 65-tile Black deck rewritten in the same task.
