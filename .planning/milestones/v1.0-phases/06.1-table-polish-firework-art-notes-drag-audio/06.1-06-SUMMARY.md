# Phase 6.1 Plan 06: Reorder and Slot-Preserving Draw Invariant Coverage Summary

Extended the existing conservation/redaction/termination property suites, the projection unit tests, and the room-actor tests to exercise 06.1-01's `reorder` action and slot-preserving draw, proving non-vacuously (via `reorderSteps` / `gamesEnded`-style counters) that the invariant safety net actually covers the new engine behaviour rather than silently skipping it.

## What Was Built

**Task 1 — Property suites drive reorder and prove slot-preserving draws:** Added `enumerateReorderActions(state, seatId)` to `test-support.ts`, which builds three candidate hand-order permutations (reversed, rotated left by one, first-two swapped), de-duplicates identical resulting orders, and keeps only candidates `canReorder` accepts — never re-deriving the permutation rule locally (T-03-24). All three property files now drive a reorder every third step (`index % 3 === 0`), picking the acting seat from `index` (frequently off-turn), and each asserts `reorderSteps > 0` after `fc.assert` so a broken generator that never produces a candidate cannot pass vacuously:
- `conservation.property.test.ts` reasserts token/card conservation immediately after each reorder, and — for every play/discard whose history gained a trailing `"draw"` entry — asserts the actor's hand index that held the played/discarded card (captured from the pre-action state) now holds the drawn card's id.
- `redaction.property.test.ts` reasserts `assertNoLeaksForEverySeat` after each reorder and additionally asserts every observer seat's projected view (own `yourHand` for the actor, `otherHands` entry for everyone else) lists the reordered hand's card ids in exactly the submitted order.
- `termination.property.test.ts` asserts a reorder leaves `turnIndex`, `clueTokens`, `fuses`, `finalTurnsRemaining`, `deck.length`, and `history.length` byte-identical before and after.

**Task 2 — Projection order and room-layer reorder flow:** `projection.test.ts` gained a `"reorder projection"` describe block: one test proves a non-active seat's reversed reorder is reflected identically (same submitted order, correct hidden/visible shape) in both the reordering seat's own view and every other seat's `otherHands` entry; a second test proves a slot-preserving discard by the active seat places the drawn card at the exact vacated index in every observer's view. `room-state.test.ts` gained a `"D-22: reorder through the room layer"` describe block covering: an off-turn reorder accepted via `applyGameAction` without changing the turn and recording `lastAppliedActionId`; a same-`actionId` resend with a *different* order deduped (first result's state and hand order preserved, unchanged); a duplicate-card-id payload refused with `card_not_in_hand`; an extra-key payload refused with `invalid_action`; and an ended room (`status: "ended"`) refusing a fresh reorder with `bad_request`.

No production code was changed — 06.1-01 already implemented `canReorder`, `applyReorder`, and the slot-preserving `drawCard`; this plan only extends the test suites that prove those chokepoints hold under the new behaviour.
