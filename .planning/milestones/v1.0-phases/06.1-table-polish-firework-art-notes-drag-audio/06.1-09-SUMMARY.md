# Phase 6.1 Plan 09: Marks Zone and Card Art Composition Summary

Clue marks (told suits/ranks, candidate pips) moved out of every card body into a fixed 28px `MarksZone` above each card, own hand and teammates alike; card bodies were rebuilt with the owner-approved art from 06.1-07 — the picture-frame neutral back for the viewer's own hand, and firework burst faces (burst-count-only rank, multicolour Rainbow, no corner numeral) for teammates — with the D-15 own-hand identity boundary extended to cover the new `MarksZone` component and the 1280x720 no-scroll fit re-verified and restored.

## Accomplishments

- `CandidateStrip` reworked into a compact two-row pip layout (`suit-pips`, `rank-pips`); told marks fold onto the matching pip via `data-told="true"` plus a `--color-card-glow` ring instead of a separate `positive-marks` row, freeing vertical space for the fixed 28px band
- New `MarksZone(facts, variant, scale, testId, noteSlot?)` composes `CandidateStrip` above a card plus a 20px own-hand note-row slot (empty now, filled by 06.1-11), structurally scoped to `facts`/`variant` only — never a card object — so it carries the D-15 boundary forward
- `own-hand-source.test.ts` extended with `MARKS_ZONE_PATH` and three new tests enforcing no suit/rank property access or destructuring, no forbidden identity tokens, and no `card` prop on `MarksZone.tsx` (closes RESEARCH.md Pitfall 3)
- `OwnHandCard` now renders the owner-approved picture-frame `FireworkCardBack` filling the card body (confirmed-glyph block and inline `CandidateStrip` removed); `TeammateCard` renders the owner-approved firework burst `FireworkCardFace` for visible cards and `FireworkCardBack` for hidden ones (inline `CandidateStrip` removed)
- `Hand.tsx` renders a `MarksZone` above every `OwnHandCard`/`TeammateCard` and merges `OwnHand`'s header into a single row
- `own-hand-render.test.ts` gains a `marks-zone-slot-1` presence assertion and a byte-identical-card-back-across-slots assertion (D-10); `e2e/start-game.spec.ts`'s own-hand marks assertions re-pointed from `own-hand-slot-N` descendants to the sibling `marks-zone-slot-N`, with the removed `positive-marks` check swapped for a `[data-told="true"]` count-0 check
- UI-11 (five players, 1280x720, no scroll) re-verified and restored after a fit regression from the new marks-zone band, by shrinking `OwnHandCard`/`TeammateCard` heights (112→100, 84→78)
