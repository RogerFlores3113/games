# Phase 6.1: Table Polish (Firework Art, Notes, Drag, Audio) - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-09-16
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Polish the Phase 6 designed board per the owner's sign-off requests (verbatim in `06-HUMAN-UAT.md`): original firework-burst card art per suit (ART-01), a static city-at-night background image (ART-02), clue marks shown above cards instead of on them (NOTE-01), private player-typed per-card notes on the own hand (NOTE-02), drag-to-reorder own hand with server-authoritative order everyone sees (HAND-01), drag-to-play / drag-to-discard with buttons kept as fallback (HAND-02), a drawn card filling the vacated slot rather than appending (HAND-03), and simple audio cues (AUD-01). Desktop browser only.

**Not in this phase:** proving Rainbow/Black end to end (Phase 7 — though all 7 suits' art is designed now, as in Phase 6 D-06), mobile/touch drag (MOB-01/02, v2), clue log / history panel (QOL-01), rematch, empathy/inference, generated raster art (possible later follow-up).

**Unlike Phase 6, the wire contract is NOT frozen.** HAND-01 (a new reorder action) and HAND-03 (draw into the vacated index) require rules-engine and schema changes. These must go through the existing chokepoints: the `GameAdapter` seam, the strict Zod wire schema, the single projection/send path, actionId idempotency, and the leak/redaction/conservation property tests — never around them. Notes (D-01..D-06) need no server change.

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Player notes (NOTE-02)
- **D-01:** Notes are **private to the writing player** — never sent to the server or other seats (no new table-talk channel).
- **D-02:** Notes persist **in this browser only** (localStorage, keyed by room + seat + card id). Survive refresh; lost on device switch / cleared site data — accepted. No server/schema change for notes. Wrap storage access defensively (can throw / be empty).
- **D-03:** Notes are allowed **only on cards in the player's own hand**.
- **D-04:** A note is **keyed to the card id**, so it follows the card when reordered. When the card leaves the hand (played/discarded) its note is **deleted**; the replacement card starts blank. Stale entries for ended games may be pruned.
- **D-05:** Edit UI: a **small note chip in the zone above the card** (beside the clue marks); click to type inline. Must preserve the 1280×720 no-scroll fit from Phase 6.
- **D-06:** Notes are **short — capped at ~20 characters** (e.g. "chop", "r5?", "save").

### Clue marks placement (NOTE-01)
- **D-07:** Clue marks (positive marks and candidate/negative info from Phase 6 D-12/D-13) move to a **zone above every card — own hand and teammates' alike** — leaving the card face for art. Phase 6 D-15 (own card shows no identity beyond `facts`) and D-08..D-10 (luminosity steps on the frame) still hold. The automatic ruled-out tracking (UI-05) remains.

### Firework card art (ART-01)
- **D-08:** The **firework burst becomes the suit symbol everywhere**, replacing the Phase 6 glyph set: face-up cards, stack heads, discard entries, colour-clue buttons, clue marks, candidate strips. Each suit's burst silhouette must be distinct with colour ignored (colourblind-safe), all 7 suits defined now, Rainbow's burst must stand alone without a multicolour fill. `apps/web/lib/suit-visuals.ts` remains the single source of the shapes (rework, don't fork). Original art inspired by, not copied from, the box game.
- **D-09:** **Rank is shown as burst count** (a 3 shows three bursts, box-game style) **plus a small corner numeral** as the fast read. Must remain legible on the smallest (teammate 64×84, discard mini) cards.
- **D-10:** Own face-down cards use a **single neutral firework-themed card back**, identical for every card (unlit shell / night motif) — zero identity signal. Known info still appears via the marks zone.
- **D-11:** Art is **hand-authored inline SVG** in the repo, coloured via `@theme` hue tokens (no hex outside `@theme`). Owner may later swap to generated raster art (e.g. PixelLab) — keep art behind a component boundary so that swap is contained.
- **D-12:** Full art applies **everywhere including stack tops and the discard pile**.
- **D-13:** Discard pile has **two views, toggled by click, per player**: **compact** (suit symbol + counts) is the **default** and lives on the board; **expanded** full-art view opens as an **overlay panel over the table** (close by click or Esc) so the layout never shifts. Toggle preference remembered per browser.

### Background (ART-02)
- **D-14:** Background is a **photo/illustration image file** of a city at night, **sourced by Claude from a clearly free-license source** (e.g. Unsplash/Pexels), with source URL, author, and license recorded in the repo. A dark overlay/dimming keeps cards and text at existing contrast rules. Static (no motion), optimized size (fits the "link click → playing in seconds" value). Owner approves the image at visual sign-off.

### Drag & drop (HAND-01, HAND-02)
- **D-15:** Dropping an own card on a zone **acts immediately** — no confirmation. Drop outside a valid zone snaps back.
- **D-16:** Drop zones reuse existing regions: **stacks area = play**, **discard pile = discard**. Both highlight while dragging; disabled zones show the Phase 6 D-18 disabled reason (not your turn, discard at 8 tokens, reconnecting…).
- **D-17:** **Reorder is allowed anytime** (including off-turn); play/discard via drag only on your turn. Reorder does not consume a turn or tokens.
- **D-18:** Teammates see **only the result** (new order / play) — no live drag streaming, no in-progress messages.
- **D-19:** **Mouse drag only** for reordering — no keyboard reorder path (owner choice; an explicit exception to Phase 6 D-19 for this convenience). Play/Discard buttons remain the keyboard fallback for actions (HAND-02).
- **D-20:** **Desktop mouse only**; touch drag is v2. Library/approach choice should not preclude touch later.
- **D-21:** When a card leaves a hand, a **short fly-to animation** to the stack/discard plays on every screen — **except for the player who dragged it there** (their drop is the motion). The drawn card appears in the vacated slot. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. This is the case Phase 6 D-22 anticipated; adding exact-pinned `motion` is allowed if CSS is insufficient.

### Hand order & draws (HAND-01, HAND-03)
- **D-22:** Hand order is **server-authoritative** and survives refresh/reconnect: a new reorder action (own card ids only, validated as a permutation of the seat's current hand) flows through the adapter, schema, and actionId idempotency like other actions.
- **D-23:** A drawn card **takes the index of the played/discarded card** (engine change at `packages/rules/src/hanabi/actions.ts` where draws currently append). Conservation, redaction, and termination property tests must stay green and cover the new behaviour; reordering must not leak identity (e.g. no ordering that correlates with hidden card identity).

### Audio cues (AUD-01)
- **D-24:** Style: **soft, minimal, plastic/ceramic tile-noise themed** — distinct cues for clue, play, discard, lost fuse (misplay plays the fuse cue, not the play cue), and completed stack.
- **D-25:** Sounds are **synthesized in-browser with Web Audio** — no audio files, no licensing.
- **D-26:** Controls: **mute toggle plus volume slider** on the board, remembered per browser. Handle browser autoplay policy (audio context unlocks on first user interaction).
- **D-27:** **Every action triggers its cue on every screen.**
- **D-28:** A play that completes a stack of five plays **only the completion sound** (replaces the play cue).
- **D-29:** **No catch-up audio:** initial load, refresh, and reconnect catch-up are silent; cues fire only for actions observed live after connection (derive from new `history` entries, not from view snapshots on load).

### Proving it
- **D-30:** Follow Phase 6 D-23/D-24 patterns: pure unit-tested logic in `apps/web/lib/` (note storage/pruning, drop-zone legality, audio cue selection from history entries, discard grouping), engine unit + property tests for reorder and slot-preserving draw, Playwright extensions for reorder visible to a teammate and surviving refresh, drag-to-play/discard, and slot replacement.
- **D-31:** Phase gate: `npm test`, `npx playwright test`, per-package `tsc --noEmit` green, plus **owner visual/audio sign-off** recorded verbatim (art, background image, notes, drag feel, sounds).

### Claude's Discretion
- Exact burst silhouettes per suit, burst arrangement for counts 1–5, card-back design, corner numeral placement.
- Drag implementation (native pointer events vs a small exact-pinned library) and drop-zone highlight styling.
- Exact synthesized sound designs, default volume, and mute/volume control placement.
- Reorder action wire name/shape and whether reorders appear in public `history` (must not be spammy; no history panel exists).
- Note chip styling, empty-state affordance, and localStorage key scheme/pruning.
- Choice of the specific free-license background image (owner approves at sign-off) and overlay strength.
- Whether a UI-SPEC (`/gsd-ui-phase 6.1`) is produced before planning — recommended.

</decisions>

## Specific Ideas

- Owner: "use hanabi's firework design for each of the tiles" — box-game style per-colour burst shapes and burst-count ranks, but original art.
- Sounds should feel like plastic/ceramic tiles being handled — soft, not firework bangs.
- "When a tile is replenished to hand, it should take the slot of whatever was previously discarded — so it's not a queue."
- Art may later be upgraded to generated images (PixelLab or similar) — keep it swappable.
