<objective>
Close the wire and UI half of UAT gap 3. This plan carries out the DESIGN DECISION recorded in 07-10-PLAN.md: the wire `StackView` changes from `{ suit, topRank }` to `{ suit, playedRanks }` (ranks in play order). The web client renders slot i of a column with `playedRanks[i]` and never looks at direction or suit to do so. A Black stack therefore shows 5 at the top, then 4 below it, and so on, filling its reserved column from the top ("Played-stack display for Black: tiles appear in the order played"). Stacks are public and identical for every seat and carry no card ids, so the per-seat redaction contract is unchanged. The leak-checker's rule that stack entries carry no `rank` key still holds, because `playedRanks` is an array of numbers, not `{suit, rank}` objects.

Purpose: remove every ascending-only reading of stack progress from the wire and the client.
Output: new wire shape, direction-agnostic PlayedStack/EndOverlay/visual logic, and e2e specs reading the new attributes, with a live Black start-state proof (70-tile deck, Black next rank 5).
</objective>
