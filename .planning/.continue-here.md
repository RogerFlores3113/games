## Critical Anti-Patterns

| Pattern | Description | Severity | Prevention Mechanism |
|---------|-------------|----------|---------------------|
| Stale dev servers poisoning e2e | Playwright's `reuseExistingServer` silently adopts leftover servers on 3100/8787. Those were started without the E2E timing vars, so reconnect specs fail and look like real regressions. `fuser -k` kills the socket holder, but the wrangler supervisor respawns it. This happened five or more times. | blocking | Before every Playwright run, kill the full process tree on 3100/8787 by PID. Leave `/tmp/games-preexisting` on 3101/8788 alone. Restart with `playwright.config.ts`'s env vars. |
| Stale e2e locators after UI removals | Deleting or renaming a component left dead testids in specs that no plan updated, which broke later plans' verify steps. This happened three or more times. | blocking | Update every consumer in the SAME commit that changes the behaviour. Grep all of `e2e/` plus `apps/web/lib/*.test.ts`. |
| Green tests, owner rejects visuals | The full gate passed while the owner rejected the board seven times: it looked tiny, the geometry reflowed, highlights were yellow, the tint was opaque. | advisory | Screenshot and check before reporting done. Treat the owner's review as the real gate for anything visual. |
| Unlicensed or people-containing images | The owner supplied stock-site previews with a Dreamstime watermark and a retailer swatch. A chosen "no people" photo still showed a person at its edge. | advisory | Wikimedia Commons only, licence verified via the API, visible credit through `apps/web/lib/image-credits.ts`, and look at the image before shipping it. |

<decisions_made>
- **Black variant (the owner's house rules):**
  - Five colours, plus Rainbow at normal copy counts, plus a reversed Black: three 5s, two each of 4/3/2, and one 1, played from 5 down to 1.
  - Black is never clued by colour.
  - The deck is 70 tiles and the maximum score is 35.
  - Direction is a per-suit property in `SUIT_RULES`, and there are no `=== "black"` checks anywhere.
  - Stacks go over the wire as `playedRanks`.
- **Rainbow tiles** get a five-colour clue row in the quick-clue popover.
- **Hints:** a colour clue shows a suit-coloured ring, and only the latest clue is shown. No yellow luminosity frame (UI-08 superseded). A number clue pulses pink (`--color-clue-number`).
- **Turn indicators** are violet (`--color-turn` #9B65F7). The turn sign sits beside the tokens, and the discard fills the lower right area.
- **Board size:** the board zooms with the viewport (`board-zoom.ts`), with 1280x720 as the minimum rather than the target. Drag deltas pass through `screenPxToBoardPx`.
- **Room lifecycle:** rooms are deleted after 24h of inactivity. The host can delete the room from the settings modal, and can take everyone back to the lobby after game over; both are enforced server-side.
- **Vercel usage:** gameplay never touches Vercel. Only `/api/room` (room creation) and `/room/[code]` renders run there, roughly 10–25 invocations per game night.
</decisions_made>
